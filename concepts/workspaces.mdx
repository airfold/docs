A workspace contains all the resources you need, including [sources](/concepts/sources), [AI tables](/concepts/ai_tables), and more.

Workspaces are commonly used to separate projects and teams, but how you organize them is up to you.

## Creation

Click on the top-left corner (name of your current workspace), then click on <br />
"+ New Workspace":
![creating a workspace](/images/create_workspace_zoomed.png)

From here, you can also access your [keys](/concepts/keys) from the sidebar.

The next step is to start ingesting data, see [sources](/concepts/sources) for further information.

## Deletion

If you want to delete your workspace, you can go to settings, then go to advanced settings, type in the workspace name under **Delete Workspace** to confirm and hit delete *workspace*.

*We highly recommend not to delete workspaces unless absolutely necessary since the data is not recoverable once you delete it.*


![deleting a workspace](/images/delete_workspace.png)
