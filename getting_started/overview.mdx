All under one roof, Airfold lets you ingest data in real time, transform it using SQL, and serve results through low-latency REST APIs.
Whether you’re powering real-time metrics, embedding live dashboards in your app, or running fast, concurrent queries over terabytes of data—Airfold provides a high-performance, cost-efficient platform built to scale. 

Unlike traditional data stacks that require stitching together multiple tools, Airfold offers an end-to-end system that excels at:

**Real-Time Data Ingestion:** Stream and process thousands of events per second with minimal latency and no batching complexity.

**Lightning-Fast Queries:** Use SQL to transform and aggregate huge datasets at exceptional, often sub-second latency.

**Seamless API Integration:** Instantly expose your query results as REST APIs for your app to consume.

Under the hood, Airfold is built on top of [ClickHouse](https://clickhouse.com/docs/about-clickhouse), the fastest open-source analytical database available. This lets your APIs query billions of rows with sub-second latency, no caching layers, and no pre-aggregation required. Just raw performance.

Airfold serves as your analytics backend—simplifying your data stack and giving your systems fast, direct access to the insights they need, when they need them.

## How does Airfold work?

Taking your project from raw data to published APIs is a simple, three-step process:
